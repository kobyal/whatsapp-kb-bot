# Getting a dedicated Israeli number for a WhatsApp bot

Researched 2026-09-10/11. Prices are list prices at that date and change monthly; line-expiry
rules below mostly come from resellers, not carrier T&Cs, so confirm by phone before buying.

## Rules that decide everything (WhatsApp Help Center)

- **Unsupported numbers:** VoIP, landline (consumer app), toll-free, premium, shared-cost. You must receive the SMS or call on the number itself. https://faq.whatsapp.com/1120385166078156
- **Linked devices** (this bot) keep working with the phone offline, but are logged out if the phone is unused for more than **14 days**; a linked device unused for 30 days is disconnected. https://faq.whatsapp.com/general/download-and-installation/about-linked-devices
- **Account deleted after 120 days** with no connection. https://faq.whatsapp.com/828406668498455
- **If the SIM expires the number is recycled** and a new owner can register WhatsApp on it. Every Israeli prepaid T&C reserves the right to disconnect an idle line and re-assign the number (019 terms; Pelephone: after one year without a call).

## The "50 shekels one time and that's it" claim

Partly true. The only genuine ₪50 one-time card is **HOT mobile HOTALK BASIC 50: ₪50, valid 180 days**
(plus a SIM). 019's closest is ₪49 for 30 days plus a ₪10 SIM. Neither keeps a number alive
indefinitely: ₪50 buys about six months at HOT and one month at 019 or Golan. Longer-lived options exist.

## Recommendation

Rami Levy blank SIM (₪6.93, in-store) plus the 12 GB / 36-month or 100 GB / 24-month bundle
(~₪140): about ₪4–6 per month of guaranteed validity, real mobile number, SMS works for the
one-time verification. Put it in a spare phone on Wi-Fi so the 14-day rule is never hit, and set a
reminder a month before the bundle expires. Runner-up for a national carrier with eSIM: Partner
Bigtalk with a 12–18-month card. Avoid all VoIP and virtual numbers.
